Welcome to Ampeah Memorial School

A Better Education, A Brighter Future

Empowering every learner with character, discipline, and knowledge.

Located in the serene township of Nkawkaw, Asubone (Whitehouse) in the Eastern Region of Ghana, Ampeah Memorial School is committed to delivering holistic, student-centered education with the firm belief that Our Strength is in the Lord. We provide a learning-oriented environment where students from Creche up to Junior High School are guided by trained facilitators toward academic success.

We believe that every child possesses unique talents. Through disciplined instruction, creative arts, ICT literacy, and extra Saturday classes, we prepare our pupils to face future academic and life challenges with confidence.

Educational Levels Offered

We guide your child through every milestone of their academic growth, from early childhood care to Junior High School graduation.

Early childhood nurture, play-based learning, and foundational care.
02

Broad Curriculum

Subjects Offered

Our curriculum aligns with the national standards set by the Ghana Education Service (GES), preparing students for future academic success.

Mathematics

Building strong logical reasoning, problem-solving abilities, and numerical confidence.

English Language

Developing effective communication, reading comprehension, grammar, and creative writing.

Integrated Science

Fostering scientific curiosity, practical investigation, and environmental understanding.

Social Studies

Understanding civic responsibilities, geography, history, and community culture.

I.C.T (Computer Studies)

Equipping students with practical computer skills for the digital age.

French Language

Expanding global communication through basic French vocabulary and conversational skills.

Career Technology

Hands-on practical knowledge in technical skills, design, and practical pre-vocational tools.

Ghanaian Language (Twi)

Promoting cultural heritage, local language literacy, and traditional values.

Religious & Moral Education (R.M.E)

Instilling sound moral values, ethics, tolerance, and character building.

Creative Arts

Unlocking artistic expression, visual design, music, and creative performance.
